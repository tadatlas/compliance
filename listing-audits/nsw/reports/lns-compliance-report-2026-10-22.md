## TL;DR

- 62 LNS listings were reviewed on 22 October 2026 (42 for sale, 7 for lease, and 13 sold, leased or off market). 1 listing had no findings.
- The sharpest issue is six leased advertisements that still read as for lease. Each shows a price guide and a Book Inspection button, while the page category is Leased.
- On current sales, four advertisements count a study, family room or home office in the bedroom total. 6/938 Military Road says five bathrooms against a count of 3, and 18 Allara Avenue is addressed as Turramurra while the description says North Turramurra.
- 38 for-sale advertisements still show Contact Agent or For Sale. They comply today and will need a price when the price-guide rule commences. Four advertisements already show a price and need an ESP cross-check. This review cannot see the agency agreement.
- First, update or withdraw the six leased pages. Second, align the bedroom, bathroom and suburb wording. Third, price the 38 unpriced sale ads and have the agents confirm the four advertised prices against the estimated selling price.
