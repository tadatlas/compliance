# LNS listing compliance report — 22 October 2026

Audit run: 22 October 2026, 5:40pm AEDT. Office filter: LNS. Statuses fully audited: For Sale (42), For Lease (7). Statuses checked for event and status hygiene only: Sold (1), Leased (6), Off market (6). No Under Contract listings. This is a triage list for the Licensee-in-Charge, not a legal conclusion and not a finding that underquoting has occurred.

## 1. TL;DR

- 62 LNS-office listings were audited. One is clean. The rest each have at least one flag. 13 listings carry a High flag, for the licensee to decide.
- The sharpest buyer-facing issue is six Leased listings whose atlas.com.au pages are still up, with a rent or “Contact Agent” and a Book Inspection button, and no leased label in the price block.
- The sharpest copy issues are hard counts that do not match the ad: 6/938 Military Road says “five bathrooms” against Baths 3, and 1/938 Military Road reads as two bedrooms against Beds 3.
- 38 For Sale ads show no price (“Contact Agent”, “Contact agent” or “For Sale”). That is allowed today and will fail the price-guide rule once it commences. Four sales do show a price and need an estimated-selling-price cross-check. None of the seven live leases uses a rent range or rent-bidding line.
- Do these first: take the six leased pages offline or label them leased; fix the Military Road counts; put a price on the 38 unpriced sales before commencement; have the agents confirm the four priced sales against the agency agreement.
